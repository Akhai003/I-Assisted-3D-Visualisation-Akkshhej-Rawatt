05 · Design Analysis

Form, Material & Visual Language

A readable breakdown of what the 3D translation preserves from the original concept.

DESIGN & FORM

Long formal silhouette with a fitted inner waistcoat and extended outer layer. Vertical proportions create a composed, authoritative stance.

LAYERING

White shirt, dark waistcoat and long coat separate clearly through contrast, lapel structure and hem length.

TAILORING DETAILS

Defined lapels, buttoned waistcoat, tie bar, chain accessory, pocket square and tailored trousers retain the gentleman vocabulary.

MATERIAL DIRECTION

Dark wool-like suiting, restrained satin accents and polished footwear create controlled contrast within the monochrome concept.

SILHOUETTE & MOVEMENT

A fitted torso provides structure while longer coat panels introduce movement and visual depth around the legs.

3D TRANSLATION

Meshy captures the principal silhouette and layered construction, allowing the concept to be evaluated beyond a single sketch viewpoint.
