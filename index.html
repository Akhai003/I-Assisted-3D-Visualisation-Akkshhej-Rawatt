02 · Development

From Reference to Design Language

CONTEMPORARY CLASSIC

The concept combines traditional formal tailoring with a darker, more cinematic silhouette. The long outer layer introduces movement, while the fitted waistcoat preserves a structured and controlled torso.

Silhouette — Long outer coat paired with a fitted internal structure.
Layering — Shirt, waistcoat, tie and outer coat create clearly separated garment levels.
Details — Lapel geometry, chain, tie bar, pocket square and cuff details reinforce the tailored character.
3D Readiness — Front, side and back cues provide enough spatial information to support three-dimensional interpretation.

05 · Design Analysis

Form, Material & Visual Language

The generated model can be evaluated against the visual qualities established by the original reference.

DESIGN & FORM

A long formal silhouette is balanced by a fitted waistcoat and structured torso, producing a composed vertical proportion.

LAYERING

Shirt, waistcoat and outer coat remain visually distinguishable through contrast, lapel structure and different garment lengths.

TAILORING DETAILS

Lapels, buttons, tie treatment, chain accessory, pocket square and cuff details support the formal menswear vocabulary.

MATERIAL DIRECTION

Dark suiting, restrained reflective accents and polished footwear create controlled material contrast without breaking the monochrome character.

SILHOUETTE & MOVEMENT

The fitted upper body establishes structure, while longer coat panels introduce movement and additional depth around the legs.

3D TRANSLATION

The AI-generated model captures the principal silhouette and layered construction, enabling evaluation beyond a single two-dimensional viewpoint.
